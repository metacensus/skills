**What a lane returns is decided by what the decision needs.** Lanes sent out before the decision is named come back in shapes nothing can compare, and the merge becomes a second round of research.

```
Decision → Criteria → Lanes → Returns → Merge → parallelize
```

## The decision

Name the choice the research serves, who makes it, and what is already ruled out. No decision, no fan-out: research that serves none returns everything and settles nothing. The decider is consulted on the frame before any lane runs, unless they stated the decision and its criteria themselves.

## Criteria

List what the decision weighs, each with its test: a **must-have** passes or fails, a **threshold** is a value to clear, a **weight** ranks what clears. Fix them before lanes run, so the merge cannot choose criteria to fit what came back. A failed must-have ends a lane early.

## Lanes

- **Known options** — one lane each.
- **Unknown options**, a wide net — two waves. Discovery lanes, cut by source or angle, return candidates; the must-haves shortlist them; then one lane per option.

Lanes never read each other; a lane that needs another's answer waits a wave. Run no more lanes than one table the decider can read.

## Returns

The frame fixes one shape for every lane:

- per criterion, a finding, its source, and the check it passed: read at source, reported, or inferred;
- the must-haves it failed;
- the unknowns.

A lane returns evidence, never a recommendation: a verdict from one lane anchors the merge before the others are read.

## Merge

One table, options by criteria. Drop the disqualified, rank the rest, and recommend one, with the runner-up and the finding that would flip them. An unknown that could flip it becomes a follow-up lane, or is named beside the recommendation. It goes up as [`sdlc`'s Roles](../sdlc/SKILL.md#roles) say, laid out per [`human-output`](../human-output/SKILL.md).

## Hand-off

The frame is [`parallelize`'s five answers](../parallelize/SKILL.md#what-the-work-answers):

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Units | the lanes |
| Edges | none within a wave |
| Action | research the lane |
| Done | its return filled, or a must-have failed |
| Checks | every finding cites a source |

The merge runs at its [Integrate](../parallelize/SKILL.md#integrate-and-land).
