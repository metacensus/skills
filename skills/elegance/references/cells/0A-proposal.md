# 0A · Proposal

Tier 0 [**Principles**](../scope.md) · [**Future**](../time.md) · below [1A vision](1A-vision.md) · across [0B rules](0B-rules.md), [0C retired rules](0C-retired-rules.md).

## Example

A proposed writing rule: the case for it, the alternatives rejected, and who has to say yes.

## Location

**Proposals**, in the [location key](../grid.md#location-key).

## Clear when

The rule in [0B](0B-rules.md) it would alter is named, the question has a binary answer, and the alternatives rejected are present; only agreement by those with standing raises it above assertion.

## Excess

- **The case argued at essay length.** Those agreeing need the question, the rule it changes, and the cost of each answer. Everything else lengthens the wait. Test: cut all but those three — if they can still agree, the rest was delay.
- **Alternatives described rather than listed.** One line each, with why not.
- **A proposal that also drafts the handbook page.** The rule lands in 0B by decision; drafting it here duplicates the page before it exists.
- **A proposal no one still wants answered, left open.** A lingering proposal is a shadow rule, followed inconsistently; retire it to [0C](0C-retired-rules.md).

## Cost

Low until adopted; then the cost of [0B](0B-rules.md).

## Moves

Sharpen the question or retire the proposal; an agent can sharpen, never decide. On yes it moves into 0B and what it replaces retires to [0C](0C-retired-rules.md); on no it retires to 0C with the reason.
