**What waits on a person is a choice, never the work around it.** A question halts only the thread it rules on, and only once the grant says it is theirs.

## What waits

The person's grant names the choices they keep. Absent one, propose this and ask once, at the start: facts only they know, canon or ontology, organization policy, a deviation from a design they confirmed, messages to others, and closing an Initiative.

- Every other choice, plan, scope, wording or order, is the orchestrator's: it takes its own recommendation, records it on the issue as `Decided, reversible:` with what would flip it, and keeps going.
- Before a question goes up, the issue, its parents and the run's earlier answers are searched: one already answered, or whose answer changes nothing, is never asked.
- What does go up goes once, as [`sdlc`'s Reporting](../sdlc/SKILL.md#reporting) says, with its options, a recommendation, and the default taken on silence.

## While it waits

Each move makes only what can be undone: a draft, a branch, a provisional pull request, a `Proposed decision:` comment. None applies `designed`, re-plans, merges what the grant keeps, or touches production or another person.

### Build the options

Each option goes far enough that the person picks by looking: the copy drafted, the screenshot taken, the contrast measured, the provisional pull request open. The pick is then a merge or a close, not another round of work. Building every option is not guessing an answer: none merges before the pick.

### Stack

A dependent whose open blockers each have an open pull request starts on their branch, its own pull request based on theirs, when nothing still pending on them, a person's choice or a contract `sdlc-crew`'s Plan merges first, reaches what it uses. It merges after its bases, rebased as each lands; a base whose review stops, or that closes unmerged, parks it as Blocked. [`sdlc-author`'s Precheck](../sdlc-author/SKILL.md#precheck) takes it as unblocked.

### Push the neighbours

When a finding needs the person's truth or policy, the work beside it that their answer cannot change goes ahead: the accessibility pass while a legal phrase waits, a notice drafted with a placeholder for each fact only they know. The merge waits, not the work.

### Run discovery

Read-only work the next decisions will need: spikes, inventories, scans, a walkthrough script for the person's return. Each returns its evidence to the question it serves.

## Overnight

When the person will be away, the run starts from a [handoff](../sdlc/SKILL.md#handoff) set before they go:

- **Grants quoted in briefs.** The handoff's Grants, quoted again in every brief beneath it.
- **Stop time.** The handoff's Stop is a clock time after which no thread starts, early enough that every running thread lands or parks at a point that can be undone before the board is due.
- **Morning board.** One page on their return, as [`sdlc`'s status outline](../sdlc/SKILL.md#reporting): their queue first, only what the grant keeps, ordered by what each unblocks; a line per thread; then what was decided for them, reversible, in terms and phrases.
