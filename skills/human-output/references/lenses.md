# Lenses

What a [loop](loop.md)'s writers build from and its critics look through. A **pellet** is two or three values from different dimensions; a **blast** is every value of one dimension.

## Organizing ideas

One per draft, each a different whole-page bet:

- around what the reader must decide or do
- one map of its structure or flow first, then the detail
- the questions a reader brings, each answered where they look first
- what changed, what it costs, and what is at risk
- the states a thing moves through, and what moves it
- one worked example first, then the rule it follows
- the options side by side
- the reader's path through it, step by step

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Values |
|---|---|
| Perspective | the person who asked, in their own words · a newcomer on day one · a busy lead with 30 seconds · a skeptic hunting what is wrong · an editor who refactors · a visual designer · a teacher · the maintainer six months from now |
| Reader task | deciding · getting oriented · learning it · looking one thing up · auditing it · acting on it now |
| Perception mode | a 5-second glance · a skim of headings, marks and first words · hunting one answer · a full read · a return visit to find something again |
| Family | status · structure · flow · logic · meaning |
| Primitive | marks · position and alignment · grouping · direction · color · type weight · whitespace |
| Component | a key · a matrix · a stage strip · a state line · a decision table · a term table · a locator grid · a tree · a card · a sample row · a diagram |
| Move | extract a repeated concept into a key, column or term · merge duplicates · move a fact next to the decision it serves · split the decision from its evidence · collapse like items into a table · layer the answer above the detail · re-project scattered facts into a new view · draw instead of tell · define a term · reframe the question the page answers |
| Failure hunt | wall of text · summary standing in for structure · retyped, not drawn · a lost condition · an undefined term · a mark that means two things · repetition · a column that never varies · a buried ask · process narration |
| Probe | would a reader pick this page or its source? · answer the key questions from a skim alone · where does the eye land first? · what is the one thing? · draw it · halve the reading load · explain it in 10 seconds |
| Stance | a minimal edit · constructive · adversarial · rebuild from the source |
| Scope | the first screen · the whole page · one section · across sections |
