> Time is the station in the loop, not the tense. A sentence does not carry its station, so mark it or gate it.

How would a disagreement with the world be settled?

```
acting or withdrawing  → A future
looking again          → B current
consulting the record  → C past
```

A station is one column of the grid: [grid](grid.human.md).

## Table 2 — Time (future, current, past)

| Station | Answers | Settled by | Confidence means | Drift looks like | Corrected by | Stale form |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A future | what could be, what we expect, what we decided to make so (the decision is a check mark on the node) | acting, or withdrawing | standing: agreed, how, still wanted; before the decision, feasibility: do the premises hold | world failed to follow · will lapsed · premises changed | do the work · withdraw · retire the option | zombie intention, dead option |
| B current | what is believed true at this scope now | looking again | evidence: verified, and how recently | world changed, node did not | re-observe; edit or delete | stale fact |
| C past | what happened | the record | fidelity of the record | record diverges from what happened | the record system, usually version control | falsified history |

Only A can hold an expectation or a commitment; the check mark separates them.

## Which station is this?

The tree above settles it. Hard cases: the shopping-list test, [theory §2.2](theory.human.md#22-time-as-the-station).

## Marking the station

| Where | Present tense is |
|---|---|
| A gate makes is and should coincide | ✅ honest |
| No gate | ⚠️ drift passing as fact |

| Station | Marked by |
|---|---|
| A future | how it was agreed, or reads as an expectation |
| B current | points at what was observed, and when |
| C past | lives in the record system, or names the record it derives from |
