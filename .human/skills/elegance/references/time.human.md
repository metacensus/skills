> Time is the station in the loop, not the tense: mark it or gate it.

How would a disagreement with the world be settled?

```
acting or withdrawing  → A future
looking again          → B current
consulting the record  → C past
```

Hard cases: [theory §2.2](theory.human.md#22-time-as-the-station). Stations are the columns of [grid](grid.human.md).

## Table 2 — Time (future, current, past)

| Station | Confidence means | Stale form |
|---|---|---|
| A | standing: agreed, still wanted | zombie intention |
| B | evidence, how recent | stale fact |
| C | fidelity of the record | falsified history |

## Marking the station

✅ A gate makes present tense honest; ⚠️ without one it hides drift. A names how it was agreed, B what was observed when, C its record.

⚠️ Cost concentrates in A, excess in B.
