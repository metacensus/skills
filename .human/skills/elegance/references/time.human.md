## Table 2 — Time (future, current, past)

Confidence means agreement for the future, recent evidence for the current, a faithful record for the past; gone stale, they become a zombie intention, a stale fact, a falsified history. Agreement is only a check mark on a future node.

## Which station is this?

Ask how a disagreement with the world would be settled: by acting, by looking again, or by the record.

## Marking the station

Present tense is honest only where a gate keeps it true. Otherwise a plan names how it was agreed, a fact names what was observed, and history stays in version control.
