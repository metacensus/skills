> A vision describes a world outsiders would call better, with a falsifiable chain from action to it; a stakeholder in the field would sign it.

```
            0A proposal
                ↑
1A vision ↔ 1B problem ↔ 1C field history
                ↓
            2A roadmap
```

Tier 1 · scope Field · time Future

## Example

The world the organization is trying to bring about · the wider movement it belongs to.

## Location

**Org Docs**, in the location key (grid).

## Clear when

outsiders recognize the world as better → the chain from action to it is falsifiable → a stakeholder in the field would sign it · confidence needs review by someone with standing plus contact with the field.

## Excess

| Excess | Test or move |
|---|---|
| **The same claim in two renderings, in different words** | One is the source, the other renders it. Replace the second with a link to the first; nothing lost → it was a missing edge. |
| **Aspiration restated at each level of detail** | World, chain, initiatives in [2A](2A-roadmap.human.md): each said once, at its own scope, and linked. |
| **Adjectives doing the work of claims** | *Transformative*, *unprecedented*: delete; a falsifiable sentence remains → keep it. |
| **A vision that also describes the problem, the organization, and the plan** | Problem → [1B](1B-problem.human.md) · organization → [2B](2B-identity.human.md) · plan → [2A](2A-roadmap.human.md). |

## Cost

A world nobody wants or nobody can reach · years · discovered only by the field's indifference.

## Moves

1. Rewrite rarely, and whole
2. Missing claim → mark it a hole, not prose
3. Connect downward → every 2A initiative traces to a claim here
4. A claim no initiative or hole answers to → retire it
