## Example

The rules the organization lives by: how it writes, what counts as the source of truth, how it decides.

## Location

**Org Docs**, in the location key (grid).

## Clear when

crisp · general · non-contradictory → a reader can name the action each forbids · the tiers below pass their checks because of it · reviewed by someone with standing, or the rule has become a gate

## Excess

The hardest tier to see excess in; this skill sits here and is not exempt.

| Excess | Test or move |
|---|---|
| **A rule that forbids nothing** | Name the action it forbids; cannot → decoration. Delete it or sharpen it until it can be broken. |
| **A rule stated, then explained, then illustrated** | Cut the explanation and the example; it still forbids what it forbade → they were residue. Keep the rule and one exemplar; the explanation is the theory's, the illustration a pointer at a node that already passed. |
| **The same rule in two documents' words** | One source, the other a link. Two repos each stating it: one is the source and the other says so. |
| **A principle that has become a gate, still kept as a sentence** | The gate refuses; the sentence is residue. Retire it. |
| **Reading order, rationale, and history in the rules themselves** | A handbook says what the rule is. Why it was adopted lives in [0C](0C-retired-rules.human.md); how the handbook is organized is one line at its top. |
| **A hole list that grows without closing** | A named hole is one line at a known scope. A dozen is a backlog misplaced; move it to the tracker. |

## Cost

The highest in the graph · every derived node inherits the bias, felt as years of drift, never as an event.

## Moves

Never extend here directly: a missing rule enters as [0A](0A-proposal.human.md) and lands here by decision.

| When | Then |
|---|---|
| Rule is loose or worded twice | Rewrite it whole, old wording gone |
| A principle now has a gate | Make it a gate (theory §4.2) → retire the sentence |
| Rule has stopped firing | Retire it to [0C](0C-retired-rules.human.md) |
| A rule is missing | Propose in [0A](0A-proposal.human.md) → lands here by decision |

Everything below connects to a rule here by name; nothing restates it.
