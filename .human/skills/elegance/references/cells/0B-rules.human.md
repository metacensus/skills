The rules the organization lives by, in Org Docs. Clear when each rule forbids a nameable action. Excess costs most here: every tier below inherits it.

## Excess

- A rule that forbids nothing.
- A rule stated, then explained, then illustrated; keep the rule and one example.
- One rule in two documents.
- A rule already enforced by a gate, still kept as a sentence.
- Rationale and history inside the rules.
- A growing list of open holes.

## Moves

Rewrite a rule whole, or turn it into a gate. A new rule arrives only as a 0A proposal. Everything below links to a rule here by name, never restates it.
