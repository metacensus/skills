Principles, Current, **Org Docs**. ↑[1B](1B-problem.human.md)

**Clear when** Each rule is crisp, general, non-contradictory, names the action it forbids; reviewed by someone with standing, or a gate. Excess hides best here: every sentence sounds principled.

|Excess|Move|
|---|---|
|**A rule that forbids nothing**|Name what it forbids, or delete.|
|**Stated, explained, illustrated**|Keep the rule and one exemplar.|
|**The same rule in two documents**|One source, one link.|
|**A principle that became a gate**|Retire the sentence.|
|**Rationale and history**|[0C](0C-retired-rules.human.md).|
|**A hole list that never closes**|A backlog; use the tracker.|

**Cost** The highest: derived nodes inherit the bias as years of drift.

**Moves** Rewrite whole; make it a gate (theory §4.2); retire a rule that stopped firing. Never extend here: a missing rule enters as [0A](0A-proposal.human.md).
