> A roadmap lists the next initiatives in agreed order, each tied to a claim in the vision or problem; the agreement is its confidence.

```
        1A vision
            ↓
2A roadmap ↔ 2B identity ↔ 2C org history
            ↓
        3A design
```

Tier 2 · scope Organization · time Future

## Example

The next initiatives in order · each with an appetite · the outcome they add up to.

## Location

**Roadmap**, in the location key (grid).

## Clear when

initiatives = the ones agreed · in the order agreed · each with appetite · each traces to a claim in [1A](1A-vision.human.md) or [1B](1B-problem.human.md) · confidence = standing of the agreement

## Excess

| Excess | Test or move |
|---|---|
| **An initiative described rather than named** | Reduce it to one line: initiative · appetite · claim. Cannot → design has leaked in; it belongs in [3A](3A-design.human.md). |
| **Justification for the order** | The order is the decision; the reasoning was the proposal's. Cut. |
| **Initiatives that have landed, still listed** | Check each one's status; landed → leaves for [3C](3C-release.human.md). |
| **A roadmap that is really a vision** | The world is [1A](1A-vision.human.md); this is the next few steps toward it. |

## Cost

A year of initiatives in the wrong order or at the wrong claims.

## Moves

1. Reorder · re-scope · retire
2. Extend → one initiative with its appetite and claim
3. A claim deliberately unaddressed → one-line deferral
4. When no roadmap exists → name the hole as a hole
