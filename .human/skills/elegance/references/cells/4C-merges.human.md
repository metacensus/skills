**Clear when** Before merge, the reviewer decides without asking: why, what approval commits to, each decision, what nobody checked. After, it names the item closed and the deviation the diff made.

|Excess|Move|
|---|---|
|**Narrating the commits**|Cut.|
|**Restating the item**|Link it; write the deviation.|
|**Reciting the gate**|The check is the record.|
|**Screenshots of the diff**|Only rendered output.|
|**Scattered asks**|Number them in one place; mark blockers.|
|**What outlives the merge**|A linked item.|
|**The author's receipts**|Cut; one line for a bent convention.|
|**Length standing in for scope**|Split where each half merges.|

**Cost** Approval without the decision seen.

**Moves** Immutable once merged, so alter before: rewrite answered asks as outcomes, link item and merge both ways, record closing status on the parent.
