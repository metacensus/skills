A pull request: open, the reviewer can decide from it — why, what approving commits to, each decision, what nobody checked; merged, it names the issue it closed and how it deviated from the plan. Without it the reviewer approves blind and the parent cannot close honestly.

## Excess

- A body narrating the commits.
- A summary restating the issue.
- A testing section reciting the gate.
- Screenshots of what the diff shows.
- Asks scattered rather than numbered in one place.
- Follow-ups that belong in an issue.
- The author's receipts.
- Length standing in for scope.

## Moves

Merges are immutable, so fix the text before merging, each answered ask rewritten as its outcome. Link issue and merge both ways.
