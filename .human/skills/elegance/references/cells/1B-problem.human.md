Tier 1 · scope Field · time Current

## Example

The problem as it stands in the world · why it persists.

## Location

**Org Docs**, in the location key (grid).

## Clear when

matches what stakeholders experience · every claim sourced, and the source says it · says why it persists → an expert who does not work here would sign it

## Excess

| Excess | Test or move |
|---|---|
| **A claim and its paraphrase** | Sourced claims, once each. For each claim, look for a later sentence restating it → keep the sourced one, cut the echo. |
| **Evidence summarized at length** | The claim, the source, one line on what the source shows. |
| **The solution creeping into the problem** | Ask of each sentence: states the world, or the answer? An answer → [1A](1A-vision.human.md) or [2A](2A-roadmap.human.md). |
| **History mixed in** | [1C](1C-field-history.human.md). |

## Cost

Solving a problem that is not there · every tier below aims wrong.

## Moves

1. Claim outdated → rewrite to what the sources now say
2. Sources gone → retire the claim
3. Each claim → connect to its evidence
4. Extend → only with a sourced claim
