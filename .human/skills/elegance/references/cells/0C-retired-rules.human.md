Principles, Past, **Archive**. ↑[1C](1C-field-history.human.md)

**Clear when** Kept as it was, plus one line: why retired, what replaced it. Version control is the rung.

|Excess|Move|
|---|---|
|**Old text copied**|Git holds it; link the commit.|
|**Long rationale**|One line.|
|**Citations in the living set**|Grep; rewrite to the replacement.|

**Cost** Low, unless still quoted.

**Moves** Never rewrite archived text; correct only the reason or replacement.
