The reviewer's stages, in the `sdlc` hub's protocol, posted on the pull request.

## Precheck

Asked to review a PR, yours only by a cited remit, not a draft, closing an open issue, passing CI, current with its base. Escalate while CI runs, on a missing request or closing link, or on a linked section its base changed; stop otherwise.

## Summarize

The PR's significant choices for a non-author, structure first: each element, or the nearest one an added file joins, its role, and what changed for what purpose. Escalate on an unexplained choice; stop on one too large to summarize.

## Focus

Fixed questions rate risk, say where to look; then `elegance` and its agent, the mapping's review checks, `open-source` if public, the host's reviewer, and the Verification. Once every check finishes, the comment is the verdict, input to the carrier's Land: pass when findings apply as written, escalate on a choice beyond the author, stop on a reopened design. Sharing the author's identity, the heading is the verdict.
