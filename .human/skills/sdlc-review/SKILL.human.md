The reviewer's stages, each run in the `sdlc` hub's protocol and posted on the pull request.

## Precheck

You were asked to review a PR, not yours or a draft, closing an open issue, with CI passed. Pause while CI runs or a request or closing link is missing; stop otherwise.

## Summarize

List the PR's significant choices for a non-author, structure first: each element, its role, and what changed for what purpose. Pause on a choice the PR does not explain; stop on a PR too large to summarize.

## Focus

Fixed questions rate risk and say where to look; then `elegance` and its agent, the mapping's review checks, `open-source` in a public repository, the host's reviewer, and the Verification. The comment is the verdict: approve, comment, or request changes.
