> Cold agents in every seat see the page, not the intent.

Fires: a page read often or by many.

ledger → drafts → pick → critics → revisions → checks → pick

## The shape

Facts tagged act · detail · context; one draft per organizing idea; rounds of lenses; the ranker may keep the current page.

## Roles

| Role | Model | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| Ledger | Sonnet | tagged facts |
| Writer | Sonnet | a page per idea |
| Critic | Haiku pellet · Sonnet blast | sketched changes |
| Reviser | Sonnet | the page |
| Checker | Haiku | lost facts |
| Ranker | Sonnet | the best, and why |

## Spending

Width over depth: 4–8 drafts, 1–2 rounds of 4. Reorganizing and implied views carry the gains; curation seldom does. Score gaps of 1–2 are noise.

## Picking

Layout, reading load and lost facts, weighed together; act facts cost most.

## Running it

`scripts/human-output-loop.js` as a Workflow, about 25 agents at its defaults; the person sees the page first.
