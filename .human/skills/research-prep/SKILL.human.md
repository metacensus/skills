> What a lane returns is decided by what the decision needs.

Fires: researching or comparing options across sources or agents; before `parallelize`.

```
Decision → Criteria → Lanes → Returns → Merge → parallelize
```

## The decision

The choice, who decides, what is ruled out. No decision → ⛔ no fan-out; the decider confirms the frame.

## Criteria

Must-have · threshold · weight, fixed before lanes run.

## Lanes

Known options → one each. Wide net → discover, shortlist, evaluate. Lanes never read each other.

## Returns

Per criterion: finding · source · check. Evidence, never a verdict.

## Merge

Options × criteria → one pick, the runner-up, and what would flip them.

## Hand-off

Lanes are [`parallelize`'s](../parallelize/SKILL.human.md#what-the-work-answers) units; the merge is its Integrate.
