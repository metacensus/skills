The author's stages, pickup to closed, in the `sdlc` protocol. A stage fixes what it can before it reports.

## Precheck

A Ready issue, or one already yours, under an open parent; with `designed`, skip to Implement. Escalate on a fix only the owner can make; stop if another's or blocked.

## Design

Body for a person, design in a comment, held to the bar; escalate until a person, or a remit, gives `designed`.

## Implement

Assign yourself and branch; `elegance` throughout, `parallelize` when too large. Escalate when the design shifts; stop when a criterion cannot be met.

## Self-review

Run `elegance-review`, `open-source` if public, the Verification, and the gate. Escalate on a disputed span or a pushed leak.

## Publish

`checkin`, link and push once handed over, open the PR, request reviewers, and move to In review.

## Respond

Apply or decline each finding, listing which and why on the PR; rerun Verification and gate.

## Land

Merge where the roles or your remit allow, else escalate, ending your run; close per the hub's Closing table, escalating only deviated or dropped closes; file follow-ups through `sdlc-intake`.

A Spike ends at its confirmed decision; an action Task hands its role the command, and its Verification closes it.
