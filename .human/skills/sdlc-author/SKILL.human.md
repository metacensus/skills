The author's stages, pickup to open pull request, in the `sdlc` protocol. A stage fixes what it can before it reports.

## Precheck

A Ready issue, or one already yours, under an open parent; with `designed`, skip to Implement. Pause on a fix only the owner can make; stop if another's or blocked.

## Design

Body for a person, design in a comment, held to the bar; pause, even delegated, for `designed`.

## Implement

Assign yourself and branch; `elegance` throughout, `parallelize` when too large. Pause when the design shifts; stop when a criterion cannot be met.

## Self-review

Run `elegance-review`, `open-source` if public, the Verification, and the gate. Pause on a disputed span or a pushed leak.

## Publish

`checkin`, link and push once handed over, open the PR, request reviewers, and move to In review.
