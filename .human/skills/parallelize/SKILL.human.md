Coordination is the price of an executor's limits: memory forces a split, bandwidth rewards one, and every split is a seam to coordinate. The skill picks the organization that needs the least, keeping the orchestrator's memory to the map, the contracts, and summaries.

## What the work answers

The work — one skill's, several skills', or a bare request — supplies units, edges, the action, a done check, and the checks that judge a unit; what it omits is derived and said. The review loop itself belongs to elegance.

## Edges decide the cut

Units split only at a contract — a surface small enough to state in two sentences; anything tighter stays in one unit.

## Owning and sharing

Writes are exclusive; context is shared, and shared context is coordination nobody has to do. A change belonging to another unit is proposed to its owner, peer to peer, and only what they cannot settle goes up. Heavy traffic across an edge says the cut was wrong.

## Decide before fanning out

A question spanning units is settled once, with the user where it is theirs, before any author starts.

## The brief

A brief opens with the agent's remit: the choices it owns, and where the rest escalate. The work names its checks; the runner picks agent or inline, and a delegated runner runs them inline.

## Integrate and land

The orchestrator decides what reaches it and owns git unless a remit hands it down, committing unit by unit only when handed the branch.
