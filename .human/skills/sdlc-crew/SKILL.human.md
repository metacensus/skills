The delegator's stages for carrying an Epic with a crew, in the `sdlc` protocol, commented on the Epic. It composes the sdlc flows and `parallelize`; the brief carries the environment and the model.

## Precheck

A `designed` Epic with at least one child, each carrying `ready`, blocked only within the Epic, no cycle; the mapping read, the delegator's own remit cited, `sdlc-family`'s questions put to the owner.

## Plan

`parallelize` over the children: contract first, pinned in a file, its consumers waiting for its merge; write-sets never overlapping; waves listed.

## Delegate

Only Ready children are briefed, and no escalation is guessed. A brief per author, remit first, in its own tree; one agent designs, and a second implements once the Design is confirmed or escalated. Authors spawn nothing; frictions return in the report.

## Review

A fresh `sdlc-review` per PR, run by the delegator, never nested; unrun checks escalate to the delegator, who takes them where the remit says so.

## Land

Each child lands through its author; the delegator takes what escalates within its remit. A Spike's decision or found work files new children through `sdlc-intake`, and Plan re-runs; a gap in the goal re-plans or escalates.

## Close

Every child closed, the rollout record, Spike decisions in the Epic, a clean sweep scoped to it, then the Epic closes completed, or not planned on a Spike's dead end, which escalates to its owner.
