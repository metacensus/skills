Files an issue and curates it to `ready`, in the `sdlc` protocol.

## Compose

Pick type, parent, and repository, fill the form, meet the type's bar. Initiatives and Epics escalate to the delegator. Compose reports to the user.

## Decompose

File an Epic's children against the plan, each blocker first, all before any is curated. Work found later files under its Epic.

## File

`gh issue create`, unlabeled, carded only where a board's purpose fits.

## Curate

Rerun Compose's checks: a pass lets the delegator, or a delegate under its remit, apply `ready`, `designed` too if nothing is open; others escalate. Otherwise, comment.
