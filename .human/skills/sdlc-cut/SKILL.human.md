> A cut carries one Publish: one repository's artifact or desired state out at one version. Minutes, one-way only where an apply's plan says so. Its yes is its Release's, or, alone, the person's `designed` on its survey; the agent then pushes the tag.

Fires: cutting, tagging or publishing a version; a person handing over a Publish; a release run failing; checking what a registry serves.

```
Plan → Change → Verify
```

|Kind|Survey|Served when|Credential|After|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|Library|API diff|registry has version|CI|adopt Tasks|
|Image|change range|registry has digest|CI|deploy or apply|
|Plugin|change range|marketplace lists it|CI|update on version change|
|Desired state|plan|live plan empty|person, production|nothing|

|Stage|Holds|⛔|⏸️|
|---|---|---|---|
|Plan|held by a Release or handed over by a person, blockers closed; commit = scope's last merge; range = scope; a named version wins, else semver, breaking = minor below 1.0.0; under a Release, the brief's version and commit; survey: notes, the kind's survey and what comes after, what the tag starts|alone, until its yes; agent-started, no Release; a stray PR|tag on another commit; under a Release, brief disagrees|
|Change|`designed` on the survey, the Release's or the lone Publish's; the agent pushes the tag; the kind's credential publishes, a transient failure rerun|no yes; a failure a rerun does not fix|·|
|Verify|served as its kind says, read with no cache or extra credentials; notes on the GitHub Release; record; close|another digest, or none|·|

Each stage checks first whether it is done, a tag already on the commit resuming at Change's run; each comment is the next stage's intent.

⚠️ Never re-tag or overwrite a served version: a new Publish retires it.
