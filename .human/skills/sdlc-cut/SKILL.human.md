> A cut carries one Publish: one repository's artifact out at one version. Minutes, never one-way: the version before stays served. Its yes is its Release's, or, alone, the person's `designed` on its survey; the agent then pushes the tag.

Fires: cutting, tagging or publishing a version; a person handing over a Publish; a release run failing; checking what a registry serves.

```
Plan → Change → Verify
```

|Stage|Holds|⛔|⏸️|
|---|---|---|---|
|Plan|held by a Release or handed over by a person, blockers closed; commit = scope's last merge; range = scope; semver, breaking = minor below 1.0.0; under a Release, the brief's version and commit; survey: notes, consumers' adopt Tasks, what the tag starts, no deploy|alone, until its yes; agent-started, no Release; a stray PR|tag on another commit; under a Release, brief disagrees|
|Change|`designed` on the survey, the Release's or the lone Publish's; the agent pushes the tag; CI publishes, a transient failure rerun|no yes; a failure a rerun does not fix|·|
|Verify|registry serves CI's digest, read with no cache or extra credentials; notes on the GitHub Release; record; close|another digest, or none|·|

Each stage checks first whether it is done, a tag already on the commit resuming at Change's run; each comment is the next stage's intent.

⚠️ Never re-tag or overwrite a served version: a new Publish retires it.
