> One orchestrator carries an Initiative from outline to finish line: a crew per Epic, no Epic, Task, Publish, Release or Rollout of its own, reading one by title and state, Plan's lock aside. Starts from a [handoff](../sdlc/references/handoff.human.md).

Fires: outlining, filing, running, orchestrating or closing an Initiative; asking what its orchestrator does next.

Stages run [the hub protocol](../sdlc/SKILL.human.md#stages), commenting on the Initiative. Crews link its Decided and Stops, never copy them.

A thread waiting on a person, or the person away: [`sdlc-crew`'s Waiting on a person](../sdlc-crew/references/waiting.human.md).

|Stage|Must hold|⛔|🛑|
|---|---|---|---|
|Precheck|Initiative open, `ready`, fields filled; a roadmap entry|not framed by the person; an unstated finish line or non-goal|Initiative closed; no roadmap entry|
|Design|one outline, blockers on their lines; an open question holds only what it rules on; filed once the person applies `designed`|the outline|a required field a hole|
|Plan|waves of unblocked Epics; overlap at each scope's grain, two Epics in one area an edge or a split; an open Release locks the environments it deploys and applies, scoped to its change; cross-Epic dependencies as `blocked by`|a contract two Epics shape|cycle|
|Delegate|a crew per Epic as a sub-agent, split at Design; grants stay in the session given; every crew and author in its own worktree and branches; each Epic framed before its crew, accepted once closed; the person steers here, plan changes mirrored on the Initiative|a Design settling a stop|a crew stops|
|Review|once Epics close, `delivery-review` over the Initiative|a deviation the Goal still needs|·|
|Close|finish line checked; every Publish its descendants block closed, and its Release; open children moved; 3C delivery record on the Initiative|always: the person accepts|·|

## Reporting

The hub's, a row per Epic: state · what runs · what it waits on; "N of M Publishes, K of L Releases closed" while one is open.
