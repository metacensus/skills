> One orchestrator carries an Initiative from outline to finish line: a crew per Epic, no Epic or Task of its own. Starts from a [handoff](../sdlc/SKILL.human.md#handoff).

Fires: outlining, filing, running, orchestrating or closing an Initiative; asking what its orchestrator does next.

Stages run [the hub protocol](../sdlc/SKILL.human.md#stages), commenting on the Initiative. Crews link its Decided and Stops, never copy them.

|Stage|Must hold|⛔|🛑|
|---|---|---|---|
|Precheck|Initiative open, `ready`, fields filled; a roadmap entry|not framed by the person; an unstated finish line or non-goal|Initiative closed; no roadmap entry|
|Design|one outline, blockers on their lines; filed once the person applies `designed`|the outline|a required field a hole|
|Plan|waves of unblocked Epics; coupling grepped; cross-Epic dependencies as `blocked by`|a contract two Epics shape|cycle|
|Delegate|a crew per Epic, split at Design; each Epic framed before its crew, accepted once closed; the person steers here, plan changes mirrored on the Initiative|a Design settling a stop|a crew stops|
|Close|finish line checked; every Publish its descendants block closed, and its Release; open children moved; 3C record on the Initiative|always: the person accepts|·|

## Reporting

The hub's, a row per Epic: state · what runs · what it waits on; "N of M Publishes, K of L Releases closed" while one is open.
