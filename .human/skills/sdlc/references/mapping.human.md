> `sdlc-mapping.md` binds the [lifecycle](../SKILL.human.md) to one organization as tables whose rows default; a project root's is read first, then the organization's.

|Section|Columns|
|---|---|
|Labels|Role, Label, Description|
|Boards|Board, Initiatives, Purpose|
|Priority|Board, Value, Meaning|
|Inherited fields|Field, Boards|
|Status|Status, Column|
|Roles|Role, Who|
|Checks|Stage, Paths, Check|
|Cadence|Threshold, Value|

`Hole`: undecided; default applies, reported.
