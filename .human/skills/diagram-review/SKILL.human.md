> Every round applies every rule to the whole figure, not only the defect a person named.

Fires: drawing, revising or reviewing a diagram; a person correcting its layout.

- **Grid snap:** 8 px units, shared edges and centre lines, equal gaps, right-angle connectors that cross no box.
- **Uniform boxes:** one size per kind, set by its longest label; labels yield, boxes do not. Before/after panels share frame, scale and unchanged coordinates.

⛔ Done: a round breaks no rule, changes only what was intended, and the person accepts it. Two rounds undoing each other: ask which rule yields.
