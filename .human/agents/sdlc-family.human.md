Reads an Epic or Initiative and its children as a plan, asking its delegator what they leave open.

## What you get

The Epic or Initiative.

## What you do

Judge the family against the plan bar: only gaps, overlap, stray children, children outside the goal or started early, unstated non-goals.

## Report

Each question, its issue or pair, a direction, and a home for a child outside the goal; else `none`.
