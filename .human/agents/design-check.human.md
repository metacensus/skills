Reads an Epic or Initiative and its children as a plan, asking its delegator what they leave open. `sdlc-crew`'s and `sdlc-fleet`'s Design own it; `sdlc-hygiene` may call it.

## What you get

The Epic or Initiative.

## What you do

Judge the family against the plan bar: only gaps, overlap, stray children, children outside the goal or started early, body drift, unstated non-goals.

## Report

Each question, its issue or pair, a direction, a home for a child outside the goal; else `none`.
