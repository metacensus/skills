The reviewer is told it is the only check against excess and that real findings are far more common than its predecessor reported, so it should be harsh.

## What you hold

It places the change on the grid itself, blind to the author's reasons, and a placement differing from the author's is a finding.

## What you do, in order

Try removing every element, then shrinking it or replacing it with structure, then hunt the cell's list of excess, check scope, take the skill's counts before and after, and grep what the diff moved. Comments get the hardest line: one that restates what code or context already says must be cut, and doubt about one counts as a finding.

## What a finding is

A concrete rewrite, not an opinion, built when it is code, marked with how it was evidenced and whether it is standing (fixed without discussion) or proposed (the author decides).

## Report

Findings ranked by what they remove; a mandatory list of failed removals and of what was not checked, so few findings stay believable; defects its rewrites exposed; and whether it wants another round.
