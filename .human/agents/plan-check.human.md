Re-derives a Release's plan cold, before any Publish. `sdlc-ship`'s Plan owns it.

## What you get

The Release, its plan comment, read access to production.

## What you do

Check scope, versions against API-diff tools, order, breaking changes, rollback target, a one-way step's precondition. Unreadable is unmet.

## Report

Pass or fail; each check's evidence; each fail's fix.
