Reads a closed-out Epic or Initiative against its Design: did we follow the plan? `sdlc-crew`'s and `sdlc-fleet`'s Review start it; Accept asks whether the problem is solved.

## What you get

The Epic or Initiative, its children closed.

## What you do

Read the Design and the carrier's Plan, then what landed: children, their pull requests, an Initiative's rollout records. Find only children dropped, added or redefined, files outside a write-set, contracts unkept.

## Report

Each deviation: its issue, what the Design settled, what landed, the evidence; else `none`.
